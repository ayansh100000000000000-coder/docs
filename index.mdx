_The Permanent Advantage_ is a workbook of positional opening puzzles taken from some of the most famous games ever played. Every position comes from a real game, and every question is the same: what would you play? You will not find mates in three here. Instead, you will be asked to judge the centre, piece development and pawn structure, the long-term factors that decide a game long after the opening is over.

## How to use this book

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up the position">
    Look at the diagram, or set it up on a real board. The side to move is shown under each diagram. When Black is to move, the board is turned around so you always look from the side of the player who must decide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a move and a plan">
    Do not just pick a move. Decide what it does for the centre, for your pieces and for your pawn structure. Write your answer down before you look.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the solution">
    Solutions are in a separate section at the back, just like in a printed book. Each one shows the move played in the game, a rating, the game line and an explanation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Not every move played in these games was a good one. Some puzzles ask you to find a better move than the one the master actually chose. The ratings are teaching judgments from opening theory and game commentary, not engine evaluations.
</Note>

## Printing the book

To get a PDF copy, open each chapter and use your browser's **Print** command (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P), then choose **Save as PDF** as the destination. Print the puzzle chapters first and the solutions last to keep the answers at the back.
