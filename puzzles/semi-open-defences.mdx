Chapter 3 of _The Permanent Advantage_ covers the semi-open defences, where Black answers 1.e4 with something other than 1...e5. The centre is unbalanced from the first moves, so pawn chains, blockades and outposts decide the middlegame. Decide what you would play in each position, then check [Solutions to Chapter 3](/solutions/semi-open-defences).
