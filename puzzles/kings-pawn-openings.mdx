Chapter 2 of _The Permanent Advantage_ moves to open games after 1.e4, where development and king safety matter even more. Some of these positions show famous opening mistakes, so the move played in the game is not always the right answer. Decide what you would play, then check [Solutions to Chapter 2](/solutions/kings-pawn-openings).
