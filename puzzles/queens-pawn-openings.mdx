Chapter 1 of _The Permanent Advantage_ covers closed openings, where the fight is about pawn breaks, doubled pawns and the bishop pair rather than quick attacks. In each position, decide what you would play and why. Think about the centre, piece development and pawn structure. The answers are in [Solutions to Chapter 1](/solutions/queens-pawn-openings).
