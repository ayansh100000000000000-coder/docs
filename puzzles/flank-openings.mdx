Chapter 4 of _The Permanent Advantage_ looks at openings that avoid the classical centre. White plays from the flanks or Black hits back with an unusual pawn structure, so judging outposts, pawn breaks and weak squares matters more than memorised theory. One of these positions shows a famous mistake. Decide what you would play, then check [Solutions to Chapter 4](/solutions/flank-openings).
